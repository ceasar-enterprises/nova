NOVA INSIGHTS / EVIDENCE FOR BETTER DECISIONS

Local evidence. Clearer decisions.

NOVA Insights is the evidence-led research, data quality and AI-evaluation operating lane of CEASAR ENTERPRISE. We help teams understand Uganda-facing customers, assess AI experiences and make evidence useful.

Our current focus includes Ugandan English, Luganda and natural code-switching across customer support, payments, delivery, booking and escalation journeys.

Responsible by design. No scraped chats or contact lists, unauthorised recordings or resale of client-owned data or outputs. Client work remains client-controlled unless agreed in writing. Any reusable asset requires explicit rights and appropriate consent.

NOVA INSIGHTS / OUR DIRECTION

Services first.
Assets when proven.

We start with focused client assignments. Reusable evaluation assets and licensing are future opportunities, not a claim that a catalogue is available today.

01 / NOW

Deliver focused services

Work with AI teams, research partners and institutions on scoped evaluation, data quality and market-evidence needs.

02 / NEXT, WHEN DEMAND IS PROVEN

Build a governed asset

Explore a limited Ugandan English–Luganda evaluation set only after repeated buyer demand, quality and rights are established, using fictional or explicitly consented contributor-created content.

03 / LATER, IF VIABLE

Develop repeat pathways

Explore repeat evaluations and approved licences where buyer needs, rights and terms are clear. Expand to new sectors or markets only with qualified local input.
