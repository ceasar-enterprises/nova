NOVA INSIGHTS / PROPOSED PILOT

AI should understand the people it serves.

We are exploring a written customer-support AI evaluation set for AI labs and teams working with Ugandan English, Luganda and the way people switch between them.

Proposed safeguards: fictional written prompts, contributor consent, separated provenance records and native-speaker review. The intended default is evaluation-only licensing. Dataset availability, delivery and licensing terms remain to be confirmed.
